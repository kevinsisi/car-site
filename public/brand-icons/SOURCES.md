# Brand icon sources

The existing ten PNGs are the partner-provided assets recorded in `scripts/download-brand-icons.mjs`.

`toyota.png` is the unmodified logo referenced by the Toyota Taiwan official homepage: https://www.toyota.com.tw/images/logo/logo-toyota.png (retrieved 2026-10-07).

These marks identify the vehicle brands; ownership remains with their respective trademark owners. Missing brands use initials alongside their full name; do not substitute a different marque's logo. The public resolver preserves configured custom icons and does not write brand aliases or vehicle data.
